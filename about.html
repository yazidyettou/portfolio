Biography

Yazid Yettou is an Algerian filmmaker and screenwriter. His work explores family and intimate tensions at the height of childhood and adolescence, in territories rarely filmed by Algerian cinema — remote villages of the Hauts Plateaux, Tuareg camps in the deep south, and border regions.

Long Bio

Trained in audiovisual studies, Yettou learned the craft on set. In 2011, he joined the technical crew of Le Crépuscule des ombres, Mohamed Lakhdar-Hamina's feature — an experience he describes as foundational. Over the following decade he worked in technical roles across Algerian productions before moving into directing.

In 2020, he directed Boumla, his first short film, shot with non-professional actors in the village of Mellakou. The film travelled across three continents, winning the Grand Prize ex-aequo at the National Festival of Women's Cinema in Saïda and Best African Short Film in the UK.

In 2025, Collatéral ! — shot in Djanet and performed in Tamasheq, one of the few contemporary Tuareg-language films — won the Gourara d'Or, Grand Prize of the first edition of the Timimoun International Short Film Festival. The film continues an international festival run, from Verona to Moshi.

He is currently developing Younès, his first feature film.

Influences — Dardenne brothers · Nuri Bilge Ceylan · Cristian Mungiu · Ingmar Bergman · Iranian cinema · Tariq Teguia
