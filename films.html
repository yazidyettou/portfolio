Synopsis

In the far south of Algeria, near the Malian border, Brahim, 10, grows up with his Tuareg family in harmony with the desert. Inseparable from his wooden toy plane, he often looks up at the clouds, fascinated by flight. But one day this fragile peace is shattered : during a hybrid military operation, a high-tech drone crashes just steps from the family's tents.

At command headquarters, the order comes down : erase every trace, at any cost. The accident becomes an invisible threat, with irreversible consequences for Brahim and his community.

The film is dedicated to the memory of artist Othmane Bali. Dialogue is minimal : the image and the landscape carry the film.

Short film · 2020

Boumla

The intertwined fates of Karim, a 10-year-old boy who believes in superstitions and absurd rituals to escape a day of school, and Lamia, a teacher whose life is upended when her father forces her into a marriage she didn't choose.

Torn between her love for Jamel and the obligation to submit to an arranged marriage, she leaves him without explanation. Overwhelmed by anger, Jamel commits an irreversible act that will change the lives of all three forever.

First film. Shot with residents of Mellakou who had never acted before, on a minimal budget from FDATIC. National premiere at the Cinémathèque algérienne, December 1, 2021.
