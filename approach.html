A Cinema of Observation

Approach

01

What Goes Unsaid

My films move through silence. What matters settles into a look, a withheld gesture, the things characters don't say to each other. I look for a stripped-down narration where the audience completes what is missing, rather than a story that explains itself.

02

Territory as Character

The desert of Djanet, a village in Tiaret, a border zone south of Tébessa : the places I film are not backdrops. They carry social and economic tensions that shape the people who live there. I shoot on location, at the pace of these places.

03

Non-Professional Faces

I work with non-professional actors, often from the regions where I shoot. What began as a budget constraint became a choice : it produces a rawness I couldn't get any other way, and grounds the film in a real relationship with its territory.

04

Withholding Judgment

My characters are caught in contradiction — a compromised father, a woman forced into marriage, a community erased by a military operation. The film doesn't rule on their behalf. It stays with them.

"I love cinema that breaks the rules."
